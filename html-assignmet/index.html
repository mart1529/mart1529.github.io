About Me

My name is Weelka Martial, I am from a small Caribbean country called the Dominican Republic. I describe myself as a very creative person full of ideas. I also really enjoy drawing and doing crafts; that is one of the reasons why I enrolled in the Interactive Media Design program. During this period, I have been exploring how to communicate ideas through images and digital projects, combining this with organizational and teamwork skills. I consider myself a curious, persistent person who is eager to learn new things every day.
