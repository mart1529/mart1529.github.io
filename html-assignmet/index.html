Weelka Martial

About Me

My name is Weelka Martial, I am from a small Caribbean country called the Dominican Republic. I describe myself as a very creative person full of ideas. I also really enjoy drawing and doing crafts; that is one of the reasons why I enrolled in the Interactive Media Design program at Algoquin College. During this period, I have been exploring how to communicate ideas through images and digital projects, combining this with organizational and teamwork skills. I consider myself a curious, persistent person who is eager to learn new things every day.

My goal is to make the most of my studies to grow both personally and professionally, and in the future, to dedicate myself to creating projects that truly connect with people. In five years, I see myself building a successful career and starting a beautiful family. However, I would like to build my own business and work independently in the field of design, offering my services to clients.
